**One thing worth handling now, not later**

Your property has a nuanced flood zone situation. The FEMA base map shows a Zone A
designation — a Special Flood Hazard Area. However, LOMR Case 20-04-4856A appears to
have resulted in removal of the structure from that designation, with an effective zone
of X (unshaded) — outside the standard flood hazard area.

Here's why timing matters: the moment a buyer or their lender starts pricing flood
insurance without seeing that determination, the word "flood" enters the conversation —
and once it does, it tends to take on a life of its own. The way to avoid that isn't to
have the letter ready in case someone asks. It's to make sure no one has a reason to ask
in the first place. Including the LOMR documentation in your showing packet and seller
disclosure from the very first showing means buyers see the resolved answer before they
ever wonder about the question. If you don't have the letter, FEMA's Map Service Center
allows a lookup by case number.
