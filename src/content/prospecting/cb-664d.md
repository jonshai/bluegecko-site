664 Doctor Ave is a 3-bedroom, 2-bath Ameron-built home in Sebastian Highlands with a
saltwater pool, a 2,076 square foot screened enclosure, an updated kitchen, no HOA,
no CDD, and immediate access to the lifestyle that makes Sebastian worth moving to.

The home expired after 201 days on market at $439,000. The asking price at expiration
was broadly consistent with where the market puts it today. The campaign wasn't built
for the buyer who actually buys this home.

That's a fixable problem. That's what this page is about.

:::

**Wow 01 — Saltwater Pool + 2,076 sf Screen Enclosure**

The 2021 renovation is documented in the permit record: swimming pool construction
at $39,900, screened enclosure at $10,550, 169 feet of 6-foot PVC privacy fencing.
Over $50,000 in permitted outdoor improvements, completed in 2021.

{{gallery:1}}

The result is a 2,076 square foot covered outdoor room — saltwater system, full screen
ceiling, privacy fencing on all sides. This is not a pool sitting exposed in a yard.
This is the outdoor space that buyers from South Florida have been paying a premium for
and couldn't find in their own neighborhood.

{{gallery:2}}

On a Saturday morning in Sebastian, it means coffee by the pool with no neighbor
fifteen feet away and no association board asking you to repaint the fence.

For the buyer trading South Florida density for space and privacy, this feature closes
the deal. The prior listing described it in one sentence. It deserved the cover.

:::

**Wow 02 — Updated Kitchen**

Gray shaker cabinets. Quartz countertops. Farm sink. Stainless appliances including
a French door refrigerator. Open to the family room — the layout works the way
open-plan layouts are supposed to work, where the kitchen is part of the conversation
instead of walled off from it.

{{gallery:3}}

Above-average finish for this price point in Sebastian Highlands. Four photographs of
this kitchen appeared in the prior listing. The photography wasn't the gap — the copy
was. The listing description never named the countertop material, the sink style, the
appliance quality, or the open plan. Buyers read listings before they look at photos.
The words weren't there to tell them what they were looking at.

:::

**The buyer this home is waiting for**

The buyer for 664 Doctor Ave is most likely not from here. They are not browsing Sebastian
Highlands because they already know Sebastian. They are arriving from a search that started
somewhere else — a search for what Sebastian offers, not for where it is on a map.

They are leaving South Florida. They want space. They want outdoor privacy. They want to
get on a boat without a two-hour drive and a parking situation. They want to stop writing
the HOA check.

A campaign built around that buyer looks different from a generic MLS listing. It names
the lifestyle before the features. It gives drive times. It frames the screened enclosure
as a room, not an amenity. It makes the no-HOA argument in the headline, not the footnotes.
It puts Sebastian's specific outdoor culture on the page for someone who has never been here
and needs a reason to commit.

That's the campaign this home needed. That's the campaign we build.
