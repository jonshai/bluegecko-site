664 Doctor Ave is a 3-bedroom, 2-bath Ameron-built home in Sebastian Highlands
with a saltwater pool, a 2,076 square foot screened enclosure, an updated kitchen,
no HOA, no CDD, and direct access to the lifestyle that makes Sebastian worth moving to.

The home expired after 201 days on market at $439,000. The asking price was defensible.
The campaign wasn't built for the right buyer.

That's what this page is about.

:::

**Wow 01 — Saltwater Pool + 2,076 sf Screen Enclosure**

The 2021 renovation permitted and documented: swimming pool construction ($39,900),
screened enclosure ($10,550), 169 feet of 6-foot PVC privacy fencing. This is not a
pool sitting exposed in a yard. It is a private outdoor room with a saltwater system,
a screened ceiling, and a privacy fence on all sides.

{{gallery:1}}

For the buyer trading South Florida density for space and privacy, this is the feature
that closes the deal. The prior listing described it in one sentence. It deserved the cover.

:::

**Wow 02 — Updated Kitchen**

Gray shaker cabinets. Quartz countertops. Farm sink. Stainless appliances including a
French door refrigerator. Open to the family room. Above-average finish for this price
point in Sebastian Highlands.

{{gallery:2}}

Four photos of the kitchen appeared in the prior listing. The photography wasn't the
problem. The copy was: the kitchen received a single clause in a 59-word description
that didn't name the countertop material, the sink style, the appliance quality, or the
open-plan layout. Buyers read listings before they look at photos. The words weren't there.

:::

**Wow 03 — Location Package**

No HOA. No CDD. Zero ongoing fees beyond the mortgage.

US-1 is at the doorstep. The Indian River is minutes away. Sebastian's boat launches,
beaches, and nature preserves -- including Pelican Island National Wildlife Refuge,
the first National Wildlife Refuge in the United States -- are within easy reach.
Interstate 95 is roughly 20 minutes. Melbourne is 35 minutes. Orlando International
is under 90 minutes.

For a buyer leaving Broward or Palm Beach County, this is a specific answer to a
specific question: where do I go when I want out, but I still need to be somewhere?
Sebastian is that answer. The prior listing never made the case.

:::

**What a BlueGecko campaign looks like for this home**

The target buyer for 664 Doctor Ave is not browsing Sebastian Highlands. They are
fleeing South Florida -- Broward, Palm Beach, Miami-Dade -- searching for space,
privacy, outdoor living, lower density, and relief from association fees that have
climbed past $600 to $900 a month in many communities.

A campaign built for that buyer looks different from a generic MLS listing.

It names the lifestyle, not just the features. It gives drive times, not just an
address. It frames the pool enclosure as a room, not an amenity. It makes the
no-HOA argument explicitly, because a buyer who has never lived without an HOA
doesn't know to ask. It puts the location package in the headline, not the footnotes.

That's the campaign this home needed. That's the campaign we build.

**Where the value sits**

Six automated valuation models place 664 Doctor Ave between $427,000 and $437,000,
with a consensus center around $433,000. The sources: RPR RVM, Zillow Zestimate,

before expiring at $439,000. The home was close to market value at expiration. The
gap was the campaign, not the price.
