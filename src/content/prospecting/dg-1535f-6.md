## The bracket strategy -- mapped to this home

**Launch:** $549,000 -- top of the $500K--$550K search tier. Fresh listing, new photography, Matterport live on day one.

**Hold:** 14 days. Track showing volume and buyer feedback, not just offers.

**Adjust:** If traffic arrives without offers, move to $539,900. This is not a concession -- it is a positioning decision. The home enters a new buyer pool. The buyer who makes an offer at $535,000 feels like they won. The seller nets within the comp-supported range.

**Do not adjust:** If no showings in 14 days, the issue is not the price -- it is the presentation or the search-tier placement. Revisit before adjusting.

This strategy protects the seller's position. It isn't about finding the lowest number the market will accept. It's about finding the entry point that generates a real buyer -- and then executing cleanly from there.
