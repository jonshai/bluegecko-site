# Avtec Homes: Production Efficiency, Custom Flexibility, Family Legacy

Avtec Homes is a Palm Bay–based builder with roots that run deeper than most buyers realize. The Sietsma family built Maronda Homes into a regional force, sold it, and then did what builders do — they got back into building. Avtec carries that same DNA: efficient processes, solid construction fundamentals, and a team that knows how to build in Florida because they've been doing it, in one form or another, for generations.

What sets Avtec apart from the production builder crowd is flexibility. Need a door moved? A wall shifted? They'll do it. That semi-custom willingness is rare at this price point — most production builders treat their floor plans as non-negotiable. Avtec treats them as a starting point.

The Gulfstream floor plan has earned awards, and it earns them honestly. It's a well-considered plan and a personal standout in their lineup. More telling than the awards, though, is the overall feel of an Avtec home — quartz countertops, tile in common areas, and one detail that's become genuinely rare in new construction: rounded drywall corners. Since LandSea's exit from the market, only Avtec and Price Family Homes are delivering that finish consistently. It's a small thing that signals a larger attention to craft.

The energy efficiency positioning is real and worth noting for buyers watching utility costs in a Florida summer.

Avtec isn't trying to be everything to everyone. They're building thoughtfully, in the market they know, with a family that has the receipts to prove they know how to do it.
