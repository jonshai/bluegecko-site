# Price Family Homes: When the Builder's Name Is on Every Door

Bill Price doesn't hand off and walk away. As co-founder of Price Family Homes, he's personally involved in every build — which, when you're only putting up around 50 homes a year, is actually possible. That's the model, and it shows.

Price Family Homes operates across Brevard and Indian River Counties, building semi-custom and fully custom homes at the upper end of the market. What makes them unusual is what that limited volume buys you: the same contractors on every job, at every price point. The craftsmen working a multi-million dollar custom aren't a different crew than the ones building a home in the upper $200s. Price doesn't maintain a luxury tier and a budget tier — there's just one standard, applied consistently because the volume is low enough to enforce it.

That's a rare thing in production-adjacent building. Most builders at scale maintain quality by segment. Price maintains it by relationship — the same tradespeople, the same expectations, the same accountability on every slab they pour.

The homes reflect it. Finishes are selected with intent, floor plans are designed around how people actually live, and the process is collaborative enough that buyers end up with something that genuinely feels like theirs.

For a buyer who wants custom quality without commissioning a fully bespoke build from scratch, Price Family Homes occupies a specific and valuable position in the Brevard market.
