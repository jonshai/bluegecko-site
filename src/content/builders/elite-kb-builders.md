# Elite KB Builders: Modern Luxury Built to Last

Elite KB Builders brings a distinctly different aesthetic to Palm Bay—one that feels more Miami than traditional Brevard. Using steel frame construction (a rarity among local builders), they've positioned themselves around modern luxury: clean lines, abundant natural light, high-end finishes, and attention to detail that shows in how you live in the space, not just how it photographs.

Most builders in the area still frame in wood. Elite KB chose steel—a decision that matters. Steel doesn't warp, twist, or shrink with humidity. It resists pests and settling. In Florida's climate, where moisture is constant, that structural choice compounds into durability. Combined with their finishes strategy, it's a thoughtful engineering decision, not a marketing angle.

Every room gets natural light. Coffered ceilings add visual depth without clutter. Walls are predominantly white—high-gloss finishes that bounce light throughout the home, creating the open, airy feel you'd expect in Miami's beachfront developments, not inland Brevard. Quartz countertops, backsplash, and shower surrounds keep the palette consistent and durable. Frameless glass shower enclosures feel contemporary without the metal frames that corrode in salt air. Modern linear fireplaces replace wood-burning clutter with glass-front design and flame behind glass beads. Details like embedded disposal buttons, separate primary vanities, lever handle doors, and shaker cabinetry signal a builder who's thought through daily living, not just the Instagram moment. High baseboards and ceilings give the homes proportion and breathing room.

What sets them apart, though, is flexibility. Elite KB doesn't just build from a catalog. Bring them a sketch—literally a napkin sketch—and they'll turn it into a floorplan and build it custom. That kind of design collaboration isn't common at this price point, and it dovetails perfectly with their premium aesthetic. It takes longer than production building, but if you have a vision, they'll listen.
