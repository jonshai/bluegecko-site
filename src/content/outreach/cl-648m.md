You have a wood-burning fireplace, a 230-square-foot screened porch, a recently replaced roof, and a renovated kitchen. You have exactly what Space Coast buyers — especially those relocating from colder places — are searching for. The right presentation changes everything. That's what we do.

## what-makes-this-home-remarkable

<label>What makes this home remarkable</label>

### 01 — A wood-burning fireplace.

In Florida. Think about what that means to someone who just moved here from Michigan or Maryland. They left the cold behind — but they didn't leave behind the feeling of a real fire on a January evening. This house gives them that. It's 55 degrees outside, the windows are open, and the fireplace is lit. That's not a feature. That's the reason someone buys this house and nowhere else.

### 02 — A screened porch that's actually a room.

Nearly 230 square feet. Bug-free. Year-round. Most people moving to Florida have a picture in their head of what evenings here should feel like. A screened enclosure is the thing that makes that picture real. Your porch is large enough for a dining table, a seating area, and still room to breathe. Buyers who want outdoor living want this — specifically this.

### 03 — A new roof in a state where that matters enormously.

Florida's insurance market has changed dramatically in recent years. Every buyer's first question — before they've even seen the kitchen — is: what's the roof situation? Yours is recent. That's a clean insurance quote on day one. It's thousands of dollars a year in premium difference. It's the thing that removes the biggest friction in the entire purchase decision. We put it in the headline. Because that's where it belongs.

## how-wed-bring-it-to-market

<label>How we'd bring it to market</label>

### Professional photography and virtual staging

Every room photographed with intention. The fireplace gets its moment. The screened porch gets shown as the living space it actually is. Vacant homes deserve staging — virtual or physical — because buyers don't buy walls. They buy the feeling of being inside them.

### Immersive virtual tours

We provide both a Zillow 3D walkthrough and a Matterport tour. Buyers relocating from out of state don't always have the luxury of flying down for every showing. A virtual tour lets them fall in love before they book a flight. That's not a nice-to-have — it's how import buyers make decisions.

### Copy written for the right buyer

The Space Coast draws engineers, veterans, healthcare professionals, and families relocating for L3Harris, Northrop Grumman, and Health First. We write for them specifically. We tell them what this house feels like on a cool January morning. We tell them what the commute looks like. We tell them this is the home they were picturing when they decided to move here.

### Maximum distribution

MLS syndication across every major platform — Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, Trulia. We also list aggressively on Homes.com and their affiliate network, which drives meaningful additional traffic that most sellers never see accounted for. More platforms. More eyes. More chances.

### Transparent pricing strategy

Five of the six major valuation tools put this home between $288,500 and $294,610. We know where the market is, we know where the brackets are, and we know how to price for traction without giving anything away. You'll know exactly what we're doing and why at every step.
